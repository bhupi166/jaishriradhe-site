---
title: "Finding Stillness in a Busy Life"
date: "2026-08-18"
excerpt: "Stillness isn't the absence of a busy life — it's a place you return to inside one."
---

It's tempting to believe that a spiritual life requires withdrawing from the world: fewer obligations, more solitude, a slower pace. For most of us, that isn't realistic — and it isn't necessary.

## Stillness is portable

You can carry a moment of stillness into a crowded train, a demanding workday, or a house full of noise. It doesn't depend on your surroundings; it depends on a habit of attention you build in quieter moments and bring with you into louder ones.

## Small anchors

A few breaths before opening your laptop. A silent "Radhe Radhe" while waiting in line. Noticing, just once, the light in the room you're sitting in. None of these require extra time — they require remembering to pause.

## The busy life is the practice

Don't wait for a retreat or a quieter season to begin. The busy life you have right now is exactly where the practice belongs — not despite the noise, but within it.
