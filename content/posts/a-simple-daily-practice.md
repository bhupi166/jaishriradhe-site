title: "A Simple Daily Practice for Beginners"
date: "2026-08-10"

---

Spiritual practice often gets postponed because we imagine it needs to be elaborate — a full puja, an hour of chanting, a perfectly quiet room. In truth, the practice that lasts is usually the simplest one.

## Start with ten minutes

Pick a time you can protect, even loosely — early morning or just before bed. Sit somewhere comfortable. That's the whole setup.

1. **Light a diya or a candle**, if you have one. The small act of lighting something marks the beginning of the practice.
2. **Chant or repeat a name** — "Radhe Radhe," a mantra you know, or simply a word that centers you. Ten rounds, or ten minutes, whichever comes first.
3. **Sit in silence for a minute** before returning to the day.

## Consistency over intensity

A ten-minute practice done daily will change you more than an hour done occasionally. The goal isn't a perfect ritual — it's a returning, again and again, to the same quiet place inside yourself.

## When you miss a day

You will. That's not a failure of the practice — it's part of it. Begin again the next day, without penance or guilt. Devotion is patient with us; we can be patient with ourselves.
