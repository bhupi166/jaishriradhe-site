Among devotees of Krishna, few phrases are as widely spoken — or as quietly profound — as "Radhe Radhe." It is a greeting, a blessing, and a meditation all at once.

## More than a greeting

Radha represents the highest ideal of devotion (bhakti): a love so complete that it asks for nothing in return. To greet someone with her name is to remind both speaker and listener of that ideal, even in the middle of an ordinary day.

## A practice of remembrance

Repeating a sacred name is one of the oldest spiritual practices across traditions. It doesn't require a temple or a special moment — only attention. Saying "Radhe Radhe" as a hello or a goodbye is a small, repeatable way of turning the mind back toward devotion.

## Carrying it forward

You don't need to fully understand a tradition to be touched by it. Start simply: say the name, mean it, and notice how it shifts your attention, even briefly, toward something larger than the moment you're in.

Radhe Radhe.
